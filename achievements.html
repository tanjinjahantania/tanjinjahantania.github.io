Research Direction

One Health and sustainable livestock systems

Tania's research interest connects zoonotic disease, public health, livestock disease management, and evidence-based veterinary intervention.

The thesis topic is especially aligned with disease awareness, farmer-level practices, and prevention-focused veterinary public health in Bangladesh.
