Image Ready Portfolio

Photo and certificate gallery with ready-made upload slots.

Add your images later into assets/img/ using the filenames shown inside each slot. Refresh the website and the slot will automatically display the image.

How It Works

Drop photos into the image folder using the slot filename.

For example, save your portrait as profile-photo.jpg inside assets/img/. The profile picture slot on the Home, About, and Gallery pages will update automatically.

Recommended image sizes

Use JPG or PNG. Wide activity photos: 1600 x 1000 or larger. Portrait: 900 x 1200 or larger. Certificates: clear straight scans or photos.
