Professional Summary

Clinically trained, research-minded, and public-health oriented.

Tania completed her B.Sc. Vet. Sci. & A.H. under the Faculty of Animal Science and Veterinary Medicine at Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University with a CGPA of 3.81 and 228 total credit hours.

Her academic foundation is strengthened by a six-month veterinary internship, international clinical trainee experience at Rajamangala University of Technology Srivijaya in Thailand, and practical exposure to digital veterinary technologies. Her interests center on veterinary research, zoonotic diseases, One Health, public health, and sustainable livestock systems.
