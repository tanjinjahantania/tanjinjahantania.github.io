Practice Workflow

How the skill set connects in practice

Clinical Intake

Clinical training supports structured case history, observation, basic examination workflow, and supervised patient-care participation during internship and exchange training.

  • History collection and case documentation mindset.
  • Physical examination exposure in clinical settings.
  • Professional communication with supervisors and owners.

Diagnostic Thinking

Diagnostic practice combines clinical signs, herd context, screening tests, and evidence from laboratory or field investigation.

  • CMT for mastitis screening.
  • Blood analysis and microscopy-supported interpretation.
  • One Health awareness for zoonotic and outbreak contexts.

Laboratory Work

Wet lab practice includes histology, staining, culture, hematology, and molecular techniques relevant to veterinary diagnostics and academic research.

  • Histological tissue processing.
  • Microscopic slide preparation and staining.
  • DNA extraction and PCR exposure.

Public Health

Field volunteering and FMD research support a public-health view of veterinary medicine, especially disease prevention and community-level awareness.

  • Rabies and PPR vaccination campaign participation.
  • Anthrax awareness activity.
  • FMD KAP study in cattle farming context.
