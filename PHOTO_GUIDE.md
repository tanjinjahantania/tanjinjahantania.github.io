## Recommended Sizes

- Profile photo: portrait image, at least 900 x 1200 px.
- Clinical/activity photos: wide image, at least 1600 x 1000 px.
- Certificates: clear straight scan or photo; JPG or PNG.

Keep filenames exactly the same, including hyphens and file extension.
