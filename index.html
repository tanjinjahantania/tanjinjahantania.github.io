About Me

Academic and Professional Overview

I am a Graduate Veterinarian (B.Sc. Vet. Sci. & A.H.) from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University with a consistent strong academic record and comprehensive hands-on internship experience across diverse fields in veterinary medicine. My training integrates clinical practice, diagnostic laboratory work, field-based vaccination programs, and academic research, enabling a scientific approach to animal health and disease management.

I further broadened my professional exposure through an international clinical trainee exchange at Rajamangala University of Technology Srivijaya, Thailand, where I gained valuable cross-cultural competence and experience with modern veterinary technologies. As an aspiring MSc/PhD candidate, I am highly motivated to pursue advanced studies and research to further strengthen my scientific expertise and technical proficiency.

My long-term goal is to contribute meaningfully to the veterinary and public health sectors within the One Health framework, with a particular focus on zoonotic diseases and antimicrobial resistance (AMR). I have a strong academic interest in veterinary microbiology, pathology, clinical medicine, and the physiology of ageing, and I aim to apply this knowledge toward advancing research, improving public health outcomes, and promoting sustainable livestock systems.
